ReGenExo: self-learning, energy-regenerative lower-limb exoskeleton

Robotics + machine learning + electronics + control

A low-cost lower-limb exoskeleton concept that adapts walking assistance to gait patterns and speed. It also explores recovering energy during negative-work phases of gait and storing it in a supercapacitor, aiming at more accessible walking-assistance and rehabilitation technology.

  • BLDC motor
  • 6:50 gearbox
  • IMU
  • Knee-angle sensing
  • Heel/toe pressure sensors
  • Encoder/potentiometer
  • EMG
  • 24 V battery
  • Supercapacitor
  • Python
  • MATLAB
  • Machine learning
  • 3D modeling
Problem and fix
The original concept was too expensive for our budget. Instead of abandoning it, I simplified it to a low-cost single-leg prototype that keeps the core ideas of adaptive assistance and energy regeneration. It taught me to balance research goals, feasibility, and real financial limits.
Results
  • 38.43 J of energy harvested
  • 1.281 W average harvested power
  • 8.2% final supercapacitor state of charge
  • Random Forest gait classification: 97.5% training accuracy, 77.8% cross-validation, 14.16 ms runtime

Edge AI predictive maintenance for machine fault detection

Machine learning + Edge AI + electronics

An end-to-end Edge AI pipeline that classifies machine health from vibration signals into four conditions: normal, ball fault, inner race fault, and outer race fault. It explores how models can be optimized for real-time use on resource-constrained embedded devices.

  • Python
  • Google Colab
  • TensorFlow
  • TensorFlow Lite
  • Edge Impulse
  • 1D CNN
  • MFCC
  • INT8 quantization
What I did
Balanced and preprocessed a public bearing-fault dataset, added synthetic data by augmentation, extracted MFCC features, built a custom 1D CNN, quantized it to full INT8, imported it into Edge Impulse, and compared it with Edge Impulse's optimized baseline on accuracy, latency, RAM, and size.
Results
  • 3 ms inference latency
  • Custom model: 259 KB, 61.5% accuracy
  • Optimized baseline: 46 KB, 63.6% accuracy

Digital active suspension control (quarter-car model)

Control systems + MATLAB/Simulink

Designed and simulated a digital active suspension system to improve ride comfort and stability.

  • MATLAB
  • Simulink
  • State-space modeling
  • Digital PID
  • Zero-order hold
  • Tustin transformation
What I did
Built a fourth-order state-space model, discretized it with zero-order hold, designed a digital PID controller, applied the Tustin transformation, and analyzed step response, Bode, Nyquist, root locus, and pole-zero maps.
Results
  • 22.5% reduction in peak body displacement
  • 2.68 s settling time
  • Zero overshoot and zero steady-state error
  • Stable closed-loop response

Auto-MPG: hierarchical clustering and dendrogram analysis

Machine learning + data science

An unsupervised learning project that groups vehicles by their characteristics using agglomerative hierarchical clustering (K = 4), plus a full dendrogram to explore the cluster structure.

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • Scikit-learn
  • Seaborn
  • Matplotlib
What I did
Cleaned the Auto MPG data (398 to 392 records), handled missing horsepower values, one-hot encoded to 101 columns, and scaled with MinMaxScaler. Made heatmaps, clustermaps, correlation maps, and MPG vs. weight plots. After normalization, the Euclidean distance between the most and least fuel-efficient cars was 2.1656.
Results
  • Key learning: unsupervised learning can reveal patterns without labels, and dendrograms help explore possible clusters.
